Operator Station Training Simulator
An independent, browser-based training aid reproducing the conventions of an Experion PKS Console/Flex Station: HMIWeb-style gray graphics, four alarm priorities (Journal/Low/High/Urgent), PID faceplates with MAN/AUTO/CAS, Point Detail with Experion parameter names (K, T1, T2 in minutes; SPHILM/OPHILM; SHEDHOLD…), alarm shelving, event journal, trends, and three live units: a continuous train with an exothermic CSTR, a semi-batch polymerization reactor with an SCM-driven sequence, and a fired-heater fixed-bed reactor. A rule-based Ops Assistant panel provides live diagnosis and coaching.

Not a Honeywell product; contains no Honeywell software, artwork, or manual text. High-priority yellow, summary columns, and key legends are representative defaults — sites configure these. Experion® is a trademark of Honeywell International Inc.
